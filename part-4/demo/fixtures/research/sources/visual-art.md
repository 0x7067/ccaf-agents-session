# Source packet: visual art

- source_id: ART-2025-01
- title: Studio tools survey
- published: 2025-02-14
- source_type: industry survey

The survey reports that small studios use generative tools mainly for quick
concept sketches and variations. It also says the final art direction remains
with a human designer because clients need a named person to approve the work.

A useful report must keep the source date and distinguish reported adoption
from a claim that the tools replace every artist. Quote this packet as
`ART-2025-01`.
